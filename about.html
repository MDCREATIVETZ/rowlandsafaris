Who we are

A Tanzania journey planned close to the ground

Rowland Safaris is a Tanzanian-owned travel company based in Arusha, the gateway to the northern safari circuit. We create private journeys that connect wildlife, people, mountains and coast.

Our role is to make the experience feel personal and manageable. We learn what matters to you, recommend a realistic route and coordinate the details so you can focus on the journey itself.
