Prepare thoughtfully

Planning matters before the first step

A Kilimanjaro climb involves altitude, changing weather and consecutive trekking days. Discuss health, training and equipment with qualified professionals before confirming your climb.

  • Choose a route and duration suited to your goals
  • Review the complete equipment list early
  • Arrange suitable travel insurance
  • Follow professional medical guidance for altitude travel
